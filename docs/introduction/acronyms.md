# List of Acronyms

Quick reference guide for abbreviations and acronyms used throughout the GIRAI Researcher's Handbook.

## Core Acronyms

| Acronym   | Full Term                                           | Description                                                                                                                            |
| --------- | --------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **AI**    | Artificial Intelligence                             | Technologies and systems that exhibit intelligent behavior by analyzing their environment and taking actions to achieve specific goals |
| **GIRAI** | Global Index on Responsible Artificial Intelligence | The comprehensive global assessment measuring how countries govern AI responsibly                                                      |
| **GCG**   | Global Center on AI Governance                      | The organization coordinating GIRAI in collaboration with partner institutions                                                         |

## Organizations

| Acronym    | Full Term                                                        | Description                                                                                                                                  |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **CSO**    | Civil Society Organisation                                       | Also known as NGOs - groups created by individuals outside governments and private companies, operating on a non-profit basis                |
| **NGO**    | Non-Governmental Organisation                                    | Same as CSO - organizations working towards social and political goals like human rights, environmental protection, or community development |
| **UNESCO** | United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization | UN agency promoting international collaboration in education, sciences, culture, communication and information                               |
| **EU**     | European Union                                                   | Political and economic union of European member states                                                                                       |

## Technical Terms

| Acronym | Full Term               | Description                                                                                                            |
| ------- | ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **M&E** | Monitoring & Evaluation | Defined processes, indicators, or tools to track implementation of frameworks and assess their effectiveness over time |

## Understanding CSO/NGO

!!! note "Civil Society Organisation (CSO)"
A group created by individuals, typically outside of governments and private sector companies, that operates on a non-profit basis.

    These organisations aim to work towards various social and political goals, such as:

    - Human rights
    - Environmental protection
    - Community development

    They can take many forms, including:

    - Clubs
    - Unions
    - Charities
    - Advocacy groups

## Usage in This Handbook

Throughout this handbook, you will encounter these acronyms in various contexts:

### Frequent Terms

- **AI governance** - How countries regulate and guide the development and use of artificial intelligence
- **CSO engagement** - Participation of civil society organizations in AI policy and oversight
- **M&E mechanisms** - Systems for monitoring and evaluating the implementation of AI frameworks

### Document References

When referring to types of evidence, you may see:

- AI policy frameworks
- CSO initiatives
- M&E reports
- Government frameworks on AI

## Quick Tips

!!! tip "Remember These Key Terms" - **AI** = The technology being governed - **GIRAI** = The Index you're contributing to - **CSO/NGO** = Non-government organizations you'll research - **M&E** = How implementation is tracked
