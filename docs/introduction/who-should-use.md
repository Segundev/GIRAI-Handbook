# Who Should Use This Handbook

The Handbook is intended for use by **all members of the GIRAI research network**.

## The GIRAI Research Network

The Global Index on Responsible AI is produced through a collaborative network of researchers, coordinators, supervisors, and reviewers working together across 140+ countries.

### 👥 Network Roles

## 1. Country Researchers

!!! info "Primary Role"
Complete the GIRAI questionnaires and draft country summaries

**Responsibilities:**

- Conduct research on assigned indicators for their country
- Identify and collect relevant evidence (frameworks, initiatives, reports)
- Document all sources systematically
- Complete questionnaires accurately and thoroughly
- Provide clear, detailed justifications for all answers
- Meet data collection milestones and deadlines
- Save evidence in the country's Google Drive folder
- Archive all web sources using web.archive.org
- Draft country narrative reports

**Key Skills:**

- Research and evidence gathering
- Document analysis and interpretation
- Attention to detail
- Time management
- Written communication

## 2. Country Coordinators

!!! info "Dual Role"
Report for their own country while also overseeing the work of other researchers within their region

**Responsibilities:**

- **As a Researcher**: Complete their own country's questionnaires
- **As a Coordinator**: Review all answers from regional researchers
- Identify relevant regional repositories
- Provide early feedback after the first indicator completion
- Lead regular debrief sessions with their team
- Review submissions for validity and accuracy
- Approve correct submissions and forward to supervisors
- Reject submissions with issues and provide detailed feedback
- Escalate persistent disagreements to the GIRAI Forum
- Monitor progress against milestones

**Key Skills:**

- Everything required of country researchers
- Quality review and validation
- Team leadership and communication
- Feedback and mentoring
- Problem-solving

## 3. Regional Supervisors

!!! info "Quality Oversight"
Monitor coordinators' work and spot-check submissions

**Who They Are:**

- Staff from the Global Center on AI Governance (GCG)
- Representatives from partner institutions

**Responsibilities:**

- Monitor Country Coordinators' work quality
- Conduct spot-checks on submissions
- Review critical questions from coordinator surveys
- Hold regular one-on-one conversations with coordinators
- Identify and address systematic issues
- Facilitate inter-regional learning and alignment
- Forward approved submissions to global reviewers
- Escalate issues when needed

**Key Skills:**

- Quality assurance
- Mentoring and supervision
- Cross-cultural communication
- Pattern recognition
- Systematic thinking

## 4. Global Reviewers

!!! info "Final Validation"
Responsible AI and policy experts who conduct the final validation of all approved answers

**Who They Are:**

- AI governance experts
- Policy specialists
- Academic researchers in responsible AI

**Responsibilities:**

- Conduct final validation of all submissions
- Assess questionnaires approved by Coordinators or Supervisors
- Approve correct submissions (questionnaire is finalized)
- Reject submissions with issues and provide detailed feedback
- Answer methodological questions via the GIRAI Knowledge Forum
- Ensure consistency across countries
- Align interpretation of questionnaires and evidence
- Participate in thematic reviews to detect anomalies

**Key Authority:**

- Only reviewers can provide **official answers** to methodological questions
- These answers are posted by the Forum Lead
- Official answers are clearly identified for researchers

## Complementary Roles

!!! success "Working Together"
Each group plays a distinct but complementary role in building a robust and credible Index.

### Quality Assurance Flow

```
Country Researchers
        ↓ (submit)
Country Coordinators
        ↓ (review & approve)
Regional Supervisors
        ↓ (spot-check & forward)
Global Reviewers
        ↓ (validate & finalize)
    APPROVED
```

### Communication Flow

```
Researchers ←→ GIRAI Knowledge Forum ←→ Reviewers
     ↓                                        ↓
Coordinators                          Official Answers
     ↓
Supervisors
```

## Collaboration Principles

The GIRAI research process is designed to ensure:

- **Quality**: Multiple levels of review
- **Consistency**: Shared guidelines and standards
- **Learning**: Knowledge Forum for collective problem-solving
- **Support**: Regular check-ins and feedback
- **Accountability**: Clear roles and responsibilities
- **Transparency**: Open discussion in the GIRAI Forum
