# Dimensions and Indicators of the Global Index on Responsible AI

The five dimensions of the Global Index, along with their indicators, are:

1. Inclusion and Diversity
    Definition: The value of promoting different backgrounds, groups, perspectives, and identities to ensure equitable participation, representation and belonging for all in the design, development, and deployment of AI.
    1. [Gender Equality (primary)](/indicators/dimensions/inclusion-and-diversity/gender-equality/)
    2. [Children's Rights (primary)](/indicators/dimensions/inclusion-and-diversity/childrens-rights)
    3. Rights of Persons with Disabilities (secondary)
    4. [Cultural and Linguistic Diversity (primary)](/indicators/dimensions/inclusion-and-diversity/cultural-and-linguistic-diversity)
    5. [CSO Engagement on Inclusion and Diversity (primary)](/indicators/dimensions/inclusion-and-diversity/cso-engagement)
    6. Equality (secondary)

2. Ethics and Sustainability
    Definition: The principles guiding right, fair, and responsible human actions, and decisions, ensuring a practice of meeting present needs without compromising the ability of future generations to meet theirs.
    1. [Fairness and Non Discrimination (primary)](/indicators/dimensions/ethics-and-sustainability/fairness-and-non-discrimination)
    2. [Transparency and Explainability (primary)](/indicators/dimensions/ethics-and-sustainability/transparency-and-explainability)
    3. [Human Oversight and Determination (primary)](/indicators/dimensions/ethics-and-sustainability/human-oversight-and-determination)
    4. [Environmental Impact (primary)](/indicators/dimensions/ethics-and-sustainability/environmental-impact)
    5. [CSO Engagement on Ethics and Sustainability (primary)](/indicators/dimensions/ethics-and-sustainability/cso-engagement)
    6. Environmental Performance Index (secondary)/
    7. Ethics (secondary)
